Why Does Most of Asia Suffer from Strong Winds and Heavy Rain in Summer?
Module 5⏱ ~40 min 讲解 + ~20 min 检测🎯 季风 · 台风 · 天气图

1. Weather vs Climate 天气 vs 气候

Weather 天气Climate 气候
时间短期（几小时到几天）长期（多年平均，如30年）
例子"今天下雨""香港夏季炎热多雨"

气象要素：

Air temperature 气温 (°C) · Wind direction 风向 / Wind speed 风速 (m/s) · Air pressure 气压 (hPa) · Precipitation 降水 (mm) · Relative humidity 相对湿度 (%) · Sunshine duration 日照时间 (hours)

2. 季风（Monsoons）

Monsoons = 随季节改变方向的风。

夏季风（Summer Monsoon）：

陆地升温快 → 热空气上升 → 低压；海水升温慢 → 冷空气下沉 → 高压。
风从 海 → 陆（onshore winds 向岸风），带来潮湿空气 → 夏季多雨

冬季风（Winter Monsoon）：

陆地降温快 → 冷空气下沉 → 高压；海水降温慢 → 暖空气上升 → 低压。
风从 陆 → 海（offshore winds 离岸风），干燥 → 冬季凉爽干燥

3. 台风（Typhoons）

Tropical cyclone = 在热带海洋上形成的低气压系统。

形成条件：

  1. 海水表面温度 > 26.5°C
  2. 充足的水汽（moist air）

结构：

Part特点
Eye 风眼中心 — 平静无云
Spiral rain bands 螺旋雨带外围 — 强风暴雨

起源和路径：

在菲律宾附近的太平洋形成 → 向西移动 → 影响亚洲东部沿海

4. 天气图（Weather Chart）

  • Isobars 等压线 = 连接气压相同地点的线
  • 等压线 密集 → 风 强；等压线 稀疏 → 风 弱

📝 检测题

MCQ
1. Which of the following describes climate?
  • A. It rained 50 mm today
  • B. Hong Kong has hot, wet summers
  • C. The wind is blowing from the north
  • D. It is 28°C now
✅ B — Climate 是长期平均，A/C/D 是 short-term weather。
MCQ
2. In summer, which direction does the monsoon wind blow in Hong Kong?
  • A. From land to sea (offshore)
  • B. From sea to land (onshore)
  • C. From north to south only
  • D. From east to west only
✅ B. Onshore (sea → land) — 夏季风从海吹向陆，带来雨水。
MCQ
3. What conditions are needed for a typhoon to form?
  • A. Cold water and dry air
  • B. Warm water (>26.5°C) and moist air
  • C. Low temperature and high pressure
  • D. Land surface and strong winds
✅ B — 台风需要温暖海水（>26.5°C）和充足水汽。
MCQ
4. In a weather chart, when isobars are closely packed, it means ______.
  • A. the wind speed is low
  • B. the wind speed is high
  • C. the temperature is high
  • D. there is no wind
✅ B. Wind speed is high — 等压线密集 = 气压梯度大 = 风大。
Structured
5. Explain why Hong Kong has wet summers and dry winters. Refer to monsoons. （4分）
✅ Sample answer:
Hong Kong is affected by the monsoon climate. In summer, the land heats up faster than the sea, creating low pressure over land. Winds blow from the sea (onshore), bringing moisture and causing wet summers.

In winter, the land cools faster than the sea, creating high pressure over land. Winds blow from the land (offshore), which are dry, causing cool and dry winters.
Structured
6. Describe the structure of a typhoon. Name TWO parts and their characteristics. （4分）
✅ Sample answer:
A typhoon has an eye at the centre, which is calm and has no clouds. Around the eye are spiral rain bands, which bring strong winds and heavy rain.
