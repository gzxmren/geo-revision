# Unit 3.1 — Where Does Our Food Come From?
## 我們的食物從哪裡來？

---

## 1. Hong Kong's Food Supply 香港的食物供應

- Most of HK's food is **imported** (入口)
- The **Mainland** is a major food supplier
- HK's **local food production** is very small

### Major Sources (2019 data):

| Food Item | Major Source |
|-----------|-------------|
| Rice | Thailand (~52%) |
| Fresh vegetables | Mainland (> 90%) |
| Live pigs & cattle | Mainland (> 90%) |

---

## 2. What is Farming? 什麼是耕作？

**Farming** (or **Agriculture** 農業) = growing of **crops** + rearing of **livestock**

Products from farms = **farm produce** 農產品 (used as food for people + raw materials for industries)

---

## 3. Farming System 農業系統

A farming system consists of:

```
[Inputs 投入] → [Processes 過程] → [Outputs 產出] → [Market 市場]
     ↑                                        |
     └────────── [Feedback 反饋] ←─────────────┘
```

### Inputs 投入:

**Physical inputs 自然投入** (natural environment):
| Input | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Sunlight | Energy for plant growth |
| Air temperature | Affects plant growth rate |
| Rainfall | Water supply |
| Land | Space for farming |
| Soil nutrients | Fertility of soil |
| Seeds | Starting material for crops |

**Cultural / Human inputs 人文投入** (made by humans):
| Input | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Capital | Money to buy equipment |
| Fertilizers | Increase soil fertility |
| Technology | Machines & equipment |
| Labour | Workers |
| Pesticides | Control pests |
| Irrigation system | Water supply control |

### Processes 過程:
Ploughing 犁地 → Irrigating 灌溉 → Fertilizing 施肥 → Weeding 除草 → Harvesting 收割

### Outputs 產出:
1. **Farm produce** 農產品 — can be **cash crop** (經濟作物, sold for money) or **subsistence crop** (自給作物, food for farmers)
2. **Crop residues** 作物殘株
3. **Pollutants** 污染物

### Feedback 反饋:
Money/cash → reinvested into the farm → buy further inputs → sustain farming

---

## 4. Types of Farming 耕作類型

### By Amount of Inputs/Outputs:
| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Intensive farming** 集約式農業 | **High** inputs and outputs per unit area; farms can be large or small |
| **Extensive farming** 粗放式農業 | **Low** inputs and outputs per unit area; farms are usually **large** |

### By Type of Outputs:
| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Arable farming** 耕作農業 | Growing of crops |
| **Pastoral farming** 畜牧業 | Rearing of livestock |
| **Mixed farming** 混合農業 | Both crop growing AND livestock rearing on the same farm |

### By Use of Outputs:
| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Subsistence farming** 自給農業 | Farm produce used as food for farmers and their families |
| **Commercial farming** 商業農業 | Farm produce is **for sale** |

### By Level of Technology:
| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **High-technology farming** 高科技農業 | Modern/advanced tech (biotechnology, machines, fuel) |
| **Low-technology farming** 低科技農業 | Simple/traditional methods; relies on animals & human power |

## 5. Example Farming Types Worldwide

| Type | Location | Characteristics |
|------|----------|----------------|
| **Market gardening** 園藝農業 | Netherlands | 1–5 ha; greenhouses, computers, irrigation; cash crops (vegetables, flowers) |
| **Rice cultivation** | Laos (upland) | 1–2 ha; family labour, simple tools; subsistence (rice for family) |

## 📝 Exam Tips 考试提示

1. **Farming system diagram**: Common exam question — label inputs (physical vs cultural), processes, outputs, feedback
2. **Types of farming**: Know the 5 classification criteria (inputs/outputs, type, use, technology) and examples
3. **HK food supply**: Know that > 90% is imported; Mainland is key supplier
4. **Cash crop vs subsistence crop**: Know the difference
