# Unit 3.3 — Can We Produce Enough Food for Our Growing Population?
## 我們能生產足夠的食物來養活不斷增長的人口嗎？

---

## 1. Why Food is Important

- Provides **energy and nutrition**
- With enough food → people can work → **economic growth**
- Without enough food → **food shortages** → **social unrest**

**Food shortage** 糧食短缺 = when food supply **cannot meet** food demand

---

## 2. Factors Affecting Food Demand 影響糧食需求的因素

### A. Population 人口
- **Larger population** → **more food needed** → higher food demand
- China's population: 830 million (1970) → 1,400 million (2019) → **increased**
- Food consumption in China has also increased significantly (1980–2018)

### B. Income Level 收入水平
- **Higher income** → people consume more **meat and dairy**, fewer staple foods (grains)
- China 1990–2019: per capita income increased → grain consumption **decreased first, then increased slightly**; meat and milk consumption **increased significantly**

| Food | 1990 | 2019 | Change |
|------|------|------|--------|
| Grains (kg) | 131 | 111 | -15% |
| Meat (kg) | 25 | 36 | +44% |
| Milk (kg) | 5 | 17 | +240% |

---

## 3. Factors Affecting Food Supply 影響糧食供應的因素

Food supply comes from:
1. **Local production** (from farming)
2. **Imports** (from other countries)

### Causes of Food Shortages:

```
Food Demand > Food Supply → FOOD SHORTAGES
```

Factors that lower food supply:
- **Natural hazards** (drought, flood, typhoon, pest)
- **Limited arable land**
- **Farming problems** (soil degradation, labour shortage)
- **Low technology** → low productivity

---

## 4. China's Food Situation

### Current Status:
- **Self-sufficiency rate** 自給自足率 of grains in China ≈ **95%**
- This means **most grains are locally produced**; only a small portion is imported
- From 2000–2004: Food supply was **slightly higher** than demand
- From mid-2000s onward: Food supply has been **consistently higher** than demand
- China is generally **producing enough food** for its people

### Future Projection:
- China's population expected to **decrease from 2025**
- Self-sufficiency rate will **remain high**
- BUT China still faces **farming problems** that need to be addressed

---

## 5. Line Graph Skills 線形圖技巧

Used to show **changes over time** (trends).

Steps:
1. Find the year on the x-axis
2. Move up to the value on the y-axis
3. Draw a dot
4. Repeat for all years
5. Join the dots with a line
6. Give a title

## 📝 Exam Tips 考试提示

1. **Food demand factors**: Population (more people → more food) + Income (higher income → more meat/dairy, less grain)
2. **Food supply factors**: Local production + imports
3. **Self-sufficiency rate**: 95% for grains in China — most is locally produced
4. **Balance of supply and demand**: Since mid-2000s, supply > demand in China
5. **Line graph**: Be able to draw and interpret
6. **Food shortages → social unrest**: Link between insufficient food and consequences
