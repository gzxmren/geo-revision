## 1. Major Farming Regions in China

| Region | Type of Farming | Characteristics |
|--------|----------------|----------------|
| **1. Xibei region** 西北地區 | Mainly **extensive pastoral**, some arable | Large farms; meat, milk, wool |
| **2. Huabei & Dongbei regions** 華北及東北地區 | **Both arable and pastoral** (extensive) | Large farms; wheat, corn, potato, millet, soybean, wool |
| **3. Huanan region** 華南地區 | Mainly **intensive arable** | Small farms; rice, vegetables, tropical fruits |

### Top Grain-Producing Provinces:
1. **Heilongjiang** (黑龙江) — 66.5 million tonnes
2. **Henan** (河南) — 65.2 million tonnes  
3. **Shandong** (山东) — 52 million tonnes

## 2. Factors Affecting Distribution of Farming Regions

### A. Climate 氣候

Climate = average **weather** conditions of a place over many years

**Growing season** 生長期 = period of a year when crops can be grown

#### Rainfall:
- Rainfall generally **decreases from SE → NW** in China
- **High rainfall** (Huanan) → **paddy** (rice) grown
- **Moderate rainfall** (Huabei) → **wheat** grown
- **Low rainfall** (desert) → crops at **oases** only
- **Very low rainfall** → only grass grows → **pastoral farming**

#### Air Temperature:
- **Huanan region**: High temp all year → **long growing season** (300–365 days)
- **Northern China**: Cold winters → **short growing season**
- Longer growing season → more crops can be grown

### B. Relief 地勢

- **Western China**: Mountainous, steep slopes, rugged → thin infertile soil → **pastoral farming**
- **Eastern China**: Plains and hills → fertile soil → **arable farming**

The height of land **decreases from west to east** (three steps:
- First step: Qing Zang Gaoyuan (青藏高原) — highest
- Second step: Nei Mongol Gaoyuan (内蒙古高原), Huangtu Gaoyuan (黄土高原)
- Third step: Plains (e.g. Huabei Pingyuan, Dongbei Pingyuan) — lowest

### C. Key Factors Summary:

| Factor | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Climate** → Rainfall | Higher rainfall → more crops grow |
| **Climate** → Temperature | Higher temp → longer growing season → more crops |
| **Relief** → Steepness | Flat land (plains) → arable; Steep slopes → pastoral |
| **Soil** | Fertile soil → arable; Thin/infertile → pastoral |

## 3. Specific Region Analysis

| Factor | Xibei Region | Huabei & Dongbei | Huanan Region |
|--------|--------------|------------------|---------------|
| **Growing season** | 7–250 days | 7–250 days | 300–365 days |
| **Annual rainfall** | Mostly **below** 600 mm | 0–600 mm | 600–1,400+ mm |
| **Relief** | Mountains & highlands | Plateaux & lowlands | Hills & lowlands |
| **Major features** | Nei Mongol Gaoyuan | Huabei Pingyuan, Dongbei Pingyuan | Zhujiang Delta |
| **Soil** | Thin & infertile | **Fertile** on plains | **Fertile** |
| **Farming type** | Mainly pastoral | Mixed arable & pastoral | Mainly arable |

## 📝 Exam Tips 考试提示

1. **Three regions**: Know names (Xibei, Huabei/Dongbei, Huanan) and their characteristics
2. **Factors affecting farming**: Climate (rainfall, temperature) + Relief (height, slope)
3. **Growing season**: Link to temperature — warm = long, cold = short
4. **SE→NW pattern**: Rainfall decreases; farming changes from arable → pastoral
5. **Key locations**: Know the major plains (Huabei, Dongbei, Middle & Lower Chang Jiang, Zhujiang Delta) and highlands
