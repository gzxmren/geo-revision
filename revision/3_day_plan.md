## 💡 Exam Tips Summary 考試小貼士

1. **Read the question carefully** — underline key words: "describe", "explain", "compare", "suggest"
2. **Use geographical terms** — examiners look for correct terminology, not general language
3. **Give evidence** — when asked "explain", always include data/examples from the case studies or graphs
4. **Draw clearly** — for cross sections, label axes, include V.E., give a title
5. **Manage time** — MCQ: ~1 min each; Structured: spend proportional to mark weight
6. **HK context** — many questions relate to HK — know examples (landslides in Tuen Mun/Tung Chung, relief of HK)
7. **LDC vs MDC** — a very common question — memorize the 6-aspect comparison table
8. **Diagrams** — practice drawing: cross section, farming system, slope forces, earth structure, monsoon mechanism
