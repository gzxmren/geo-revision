1. 地球结构（Structure of the Earth）

Layer厚度特点
Crust 地殼6–70 km刚性岩石，构成大陆和洋底
Mantle 地幔~2,900 km密度较大，高温高压
Core 地核~3,470 km密度最大，温度压力最高
  • Lithosphere 岩石圈 = Crust + 地幔顶部（刚性固体）
  • Asthenosphere 软流圈 = 地幔上层（塑性，缓慢流动）
  • Plates 板块 = 岩石圈分裂成的块，在软流圈上"漂浮"

2. 板块运动与地震

板块每年移动几厘米。三种方式：

Movement方向
Moving apart 分离远离
Moving towards 汇聚靠近
Moving past 平移擦过

地震如何发生：

板块移动 → 岩石受力 → 能量累积 → 能量超过岩石强度 → 岩石断裂 → 能量释放 → 地震！

Focus 震源 = 地下的断裂点  |  Epicentre 震央 = 地面正上方
~90% 的地震发生在板块边界附近

3. 测量地震

Scale测量什么
Richter scale 黎克特震级Magnitude — 释放的能量大小
Modified Mercalli Intensity 修订麦加利震级Intensity — 造成的破坏程度

4. 地震的影响

Direct / Primary 直接影响：

  • 建筑物倒塌
  • 交通系统损坏
  • 煤气水管断裂

Indirect / Secondary 间接影响：

  • 山泥倾泻（由地震引发）
  • Tsunami 海啸（海底地震引发）
  • 洪水（水坝倒塌）
  • 火灾
  • 疾病传播

Preventive 预防（事前）Remedial 补救（事后）

📝 检测题

MCQ
1. Which layer of the earth is the thinnest?
  • A. Core
  • B. Mantle
  • C. Crust
  • D. Asthenosphere
✅ C. Crust（地壳） — 仅6–70 km厚。
MCQ
2. Where do most earthquakes occur?
  • A. In the middle of oceans
  • B. Near plate boundaries
  • C. In polar regions
  • D. At the equator
✅ B. Near plate boundaries — 约90%地震发生在板块边界附近。
MCQ
3. Which of the following is an indirect effect of an earthquake?
  • A. Collapse of buildings
  • B. Breaking of power lines
  • C. Tsunami
  • D. Damage to roads
✅ C. Tsunami（海啸） — 是地震的次生/间接影响。A/B/D 都是直接效果。
MCQ
4. The Richter scale measures the ______ of an earthquake.
  • A. intensity (damage caused)
  • B. magnitude (energy released)
  • C. duration
  • D. depth
✅ B. Magnitude（能量） — Richter = 震级大小；MMI = 破坏程度。
Structured
5. Name TWO effects of the Wenchuan earthquake. （2分）
✅ Over 69,000 people died. Landslides blocked rivers and formed lakes. Most roads and houses within 100,000 km² were damaged.
Structured
6. Explain ONE preventive measure and ONE remedial measure for earthquakes. （4分）
✅ Sample answer:
Preventive: Constructing earthquake-proof buildings prevents collapse and reduces loss of life.
Remedial: Having well-trained rescue teams increases the number of survivors after an earthquake.
