1. 中国三⼤农业区

Region位置耕作类型特点
1. Xibei region 西北地区西北部Mainly extensive pastoral，少量 arable大型农场；肉、奶、羊毛
2. Huabei & Dongbei 华北及东北地区北部和东北Both arable and pastoral（extensive）大型农场；小麦、玉米、土豆、大豆
3. Huanan region 华南地区南部Mainly intensive arable小型农场；水稻、蔬菜、热带水果

Top 3 产粮大省：

  1. Heilongjiang 黑龙江 — 66.5百万吨
  2. Henan 河南 — 65.2百万吨
  3. Shandong 山东 — 52百万吨

2. 影响农业分布的因素

A. Climate 气候

Growing season 生长期 = 一年中作物可以生长的时期

Rainfall 降水：从东南向西北递减（SE→NW）

降雨量适合的农业
高（华南）水稻 paddy
中等（华北）小麦 wheat
低（西北）绿洲 oasis 或 畜牧 pastoral

Temperature 温度：华南全年高温 → 长生长期（300–365天）；北方冬季寒冷 → 生长期短

B. Relief 地势

  • 西部：山地、陡坡 → 土壤贫瘠 → pastoral farming
  • 东部：平原、丘陵 → 土壤肥沃 → arable farming
  • 中国地势从西向东三级下降：青藏高原 → 内蒙古/黄土高原 → 平原

MCQ
1. Which region in China has the longest growing season?
  • A. Xibei region
  • B. Huabei region
  • C. Huanan region
  • D. Dongbei region
✅ C. Huanan region — 华南高温全年，生长期300–365天。
MCQ
2. Which factor explains why Xibei region mainly practises pastoral farming?
  • A. High rainfall and flat land
  • B. Low rainfall and mountainous relief
  • C. Long growing season and fertile soil
  • D. High population density
✅ B — 西北降水量低(<600mm)、多山地高原 → 只能长草 → 适合畜牧。
Fill-in
3. The period of a year when crops can be grown is called the  .
✅ growing season（生长期）
Structured
4. Explain how climate and relief affect the distribution of farming regions in China. （4分）
✅ Sample answer:
Climate: Rainfall decreases from SE to NW. Huanan region (high rainfall) grows paddy, while Xibei region (low rainfall) mainly practises pastoral farming. Temperature also affects the growing season — longer in the south (365 days) and shorter in the north.
Relief: Western China is mountainous with thin soil → pastoral farming. Eastern China has plains with fertile soil → arable farming.
