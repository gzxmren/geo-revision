Unit 3.3 — Can We Produce Enough Food?

1. 为什么粮食重要？

  • 提供能量和营养
  • 粮食充足 → 经济发展
  • 粮食短缺 → 社会动荡

2. 影响粮食需求的因素

Factor影响
Population 人口人口越多 → 粮食需求越大
Income level 收入水平收入越高 → 吃肉/奶越多，吃谷物越少

3. 影响粮食供应的因素

粮食供应来自：Local production 本地生产 + Imports 进口

粮食短缺 = Food Demand > Food Supply

4. 中国的粮食状况

  • Self-sufficiency rate 自给自足率 of grains ≈ 95%
  • 大部分谷物本地生产，只有少量进口
  • 2000–2004年：供应略高于需求
  • 2000年代中期后：供应持续高于需求
  • 预计2025年后人口下降 → 自给率将保持高位

Section B: Structured (2题, 每题5分)

Structured
11. Describe the climatic characteristics of Hong Kong. Refer to the monsoon mechanism in your answer. （5分）
✅ Sample answer: Hong Kong has a monsoon climate with four distinct seasons. Summers are hot and wet because the summer monsoon brings onshore winds from the sea, carrying moisture. Winters are cool and dry because the winter monsoon blows from the land (offshore winds), which are dry. Spring is mild and humid, autumn is warm and dry. The annual mean temperature is about 23°C, and the annual rainfall is over 2,200 mm, most of which falls in summer.
Structured
12. Explain how ONE physical factor (climate or relief) affects the distribution of farming regions in China. Use examples. （5分）
✅ Sample answer: Climate (rainfall) is a key factor affecting farming distribution in China. Rainfall generally decreases from the southeast to the northwest. In Huanan region (southeast), annual rainfall is over 1,000 mm, which is suitable for growing paddy (rice). In Xibei region (northwest), rainfall is mostly below 600 mm, so only grass can grow, and pastoral farming is practised. In Huabei region, moderate rainfall allows wheat to be grown.
