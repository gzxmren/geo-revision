1. 香港的食物供应

香港大部分食物依赖 import（入口），本地生产很少。

Food主要来源
Rice 米Thailand 泰国（~52%）
Fresh vegetables 新鲜蔬菜The Mainland 内地（>90%）
Live pigs & cattle 活猪牛The Mainland 内地（>90%）

2. 什么是 Farming（农业）？

Farming / Agriculture = growing of crops（作物） + rearing of livestock（牲畜）

农场产品叫 farm produce（农产品），用作 food（食物） 和 raw materials（原料）。

3. Farming System（农业系统）

[Inputs 投入] → [Processes 过程] → [Outputs 产出] → [Market 市场]
    ↑                              │
    └────── [Feedback 反馈] ←─────────┘

Inputs 投入：

Physical inputs 自然投入Cultural / Human inputs 人文投入
Sunlight 阳光Capital 资金
Air temperature 气温Fertilizers 肥料
Rainfall 降水Technology 科技
Land 土地Labour 劳力
Soil nutrients 土壤养分Pesticides 杀虫剂
Seeds 种子Irrigation system 灌溉系统

Processes 过程：

Ploughing 犁地 → Irrigating 灌溉 → Fertilizing 施肥 → Weeding 除草 → Harvesting 收割

Outputs 产出：

  • Farm produce 农产品 — 分为 cash crop 经济作物（卖钱）和 subsistence crop 自给作物（自己吃）
  • Crop residues 作物残株
  • Pollutants 污染物

Feedback 反馈：

卖货得到的 money/cash → reinvest 再投入农场 → 维持耕作

4. Types of Farming（耕作类型）

按投入/产出量

Type中文特点
Intensive farming集约式农业单位面积高投入高产出；可大可小
Extensive farming粗放式农业单位面积低投入低产出；通常很大

按产出类型

Type中文特点
Arable farming耕作农业种庄稼
Pastoral farming畜牧业养牲畜
Mixed farming混合农业种 + 养

按产出用途

Type中文特点
Subsistence farming自给农业自己吃
Commercial farming商业农业卖钱

按科技水平

Type中文特点
High-technology farming高科技农业用机械、生物科技
Low-technology farming低科技农业用人力、畜力

3. Which of the following is a cultural / human input in a farming system?
  • A. Sunlight
  • B. Rainfall
  • C. Pesticides
  • D. Soil nutrients
✅ C. Pesticides（杀虫剂） — 人类制造的，属于 cultural input。A/B/D 都是 physical（自然）的。
MCQ
4. A farm with high inputs and outputs per unit area is called ______.
  • A. extensive farming
  • B. subsistence farming
  • C. intensive farming
  • D. low-technology farming
✅ C. intensive farming（集约式农业）
Classify
5. Classify: (a) Growing crops only →   (b) Rearing livestock only →   (c) Crops + livestock together →  
✅ (a) Arable farming / (b) Pastoral farming / (c) Mixed farming
Structured
6. Draw a labelled farming system diagram for a vegetable farm. Name TWO physical inputs and TWO cultural inputs. （4分）
✅ Sample answer:
Physical inputs: Sunlight, rainfall
Cultural inputs: Labour (workers), fertilizers
Processes: Ploughing → Irrigating → Fertilizing → Weeding → Harvesting
Outputs: Farm produce (cash crops + subsistence crops), crop residues, pollutants
Feedback: Cash from market → reinvest in farm
Structured
7. Compare intensive farming and extensive farming. Give ONE difference and ONE example for each. （4分）
✅ Sample answer:
Intensive farming has high inputs and outputs per unit area of land. Labour and capital inputs are high. An example is market gardening in the Netherlands (1–5 ha farms with greenhouses).

Extensive farming has low inputs and outputs per unit area of land. Farms are usually large. An example is wheat farming in the USA (400 ha farm with very few workers per hectare).
