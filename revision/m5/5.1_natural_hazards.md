## 1. Natural Hazards 自然災害

### Key Concepts 核心概念

| English | 中文 | Definition |
|---------|------|------------|
| Natural event | 自然事件 | A naturally occurring event caused by natural processes (e.g. volcanic eruption) |
| **Natural hazard** | **自然災害** | A natural event that **causes harm and loss** to humans (threatens life & property) |
| **Natural disaster** | **自然災難** | When a natural hazard causes **great loss of life and severe property damage** |

> **Difference**: Natural event → Natural hazard (when it affects people) → Natural disaster (when it causes massive damage)

### Classification of Natural Hazards 分类

| Type 类型 | Cause 成因 | Examples 例子 |
|-----------|-----------|--------------|
| **Geological hazard** 地質災害 | Geological processes / earth movement | Volcanic eruption 火山爆發, **Landslide 山泥傾瀉**, **Earthquake 地震** |
| **Meteorological hazard** 氣象災害 | Extreme weather or climate | Drought 乾旱, Flood 氾濫, **Typhoon 颱風** |
| **Biological hazard** 生物災害 | Living organisms | Pest 蟲害 (e.g. plague of locusts 蝗禍) |

---

## 2. Global Distribution 全球分布

### Key Distribution Patterns

| Hazard | Distribution |
|--------|-------------|
| Earthquakes | Along **earthquake belts** (plate boundaries), e.g. Pacific Ring of Fire |
| Volcanic eruptions | Similar to earthquake belts; linear/clustered pattern around Pacific Ocean |
| Typhoons | **Tropical regions** (23.5°N to 23.5°S), mainly over warm oceans |
| Floods | Near rivers and coastal areas |
| Drought | Continental interiors, dry regions |

**Key Observation**: Distribution is **uneven** — few places are free of all natural hazards.

### GIS Application
- Geographic Information System (GIS) can show distribution of natural hazards in **layers** on a map

## 📝 Exam Tips 考试提示

1. **Know the classification**: Geological vs Meteorological vs Biological — give examples for each
2. **Natural event → hazard → disaster**: Be able to explain the difference in sequence
3. **Uneven distribution**: This phrase appears frequently — remember it's the key descriptor
4. **Hong Kong context**: HK has typhoons and landslides but NOT volcanic eruptions or earthquakes
