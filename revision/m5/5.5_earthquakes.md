# Unit 5.5 — Why Does Our Land Shake Violently?
## 為什麼我們的大地會猛烈搖晃？

---

## 1. What is an Earthquake?

Earthquake = sudden **shaking of the ground** caused by the **release of stored energy** in rocks when they break.

---

## 2. Structure of the Earth 地球結構

| Layer | Thickness | Characteristics |
|-------|-----------|----------------|
| **Crust** 地殼 | 6–70 km | Rigid rocks; forms continents and ocean floors |
| **Mantle** 地幔 | ~2,900 km | Denser rocks; high temperature and pressure |
| **Core** 地核 | ~3,470 km | Densest rocks; highest temperature and pressure |

- **Lithosphere** 岩石圈 = Crust + uppermost part of mantle (**rigid**, solid)
- **Asthenosphere** 軟流圈 = Upper part of mantle below lithosphere (**plastic**, flows slowly)
- **Plates** 板塊 = Pieces of lithosphere that "float" on asthenosphere

---

## 3. Plate Movement 板塊移動

Plates move **slowly** (a few cm per year) in three ways:

| Movement | Direction | Example Boundary |
|----------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Moving apart** (divergent) | Away from each other | Mid-Atlantic Ridge |
| **Moving towards** (convergent) | Towards each other | Pacific Plate → Eurasian Plate |
| **Moving past** (transform) | Sliding past each other | San Andreas Fault |

---

## 4. Plate Boundaries and Earthquakes

- **Plate boundary** 板塊邊界 = junction between two moving plates
- **~90% of earthquakes** occur near plate boundaries
- **Focus** 震源 = point **underground** where earthquake originates
- **Epicentre** 震央 = point on **ground surface** directly above focus
- Energy released as **seismic waves** 地震波

### How Earthquakes Occur:

```
Plates move → Rock experiences stress → Energy builds up in rock
→ Energy exceeds rock strength → Rock breaks → Energy released
→ Ground shakes (EARTHQUAKE!)
```

---

## 5. Measuring Earthquakes

| Scale | What It Measures | Description |
|-------|-----------------|-------------|
| **Richter scale** 黎克特震級 | **Magnitude** — amount of energy released | < 3.0 (not felt) → 9+ (total destruction) |
| **Modified Mercalli Intensity (MMI) Scale** 修訂麥加利震級 | **Intensity** — damage caused | I (not felt) → XII (total destruction) |

---

## 6. Effects of Earthquakes

### Direct (Primary) Effects:
- Collapse of buildings / structures
- Damage to transport systems (roads, bridges)
- Breaking of power lines, gas and water pipes

### Indirect (Secondary) Effects:
- Landslides (triggered by shaking)
- Tsunamis (if earthquake occurs under the sea)
- Floods (if dams break)
- Fires (from broken gas pipes / power lines)
- Disruption to delivery of emergency aid
- Spread of disease

---

## 7. Case Study: Wenchuan Earthquake 2008

| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|
| Location | **Wenchuan**, Sichuan, China |
| Magnitude | **8.0** |
| Deaths | > 69,000 |
| Injuries | > 370,000 |
| Missing | ~ 18,000 |
| Damage | Most roads/houses damaged within 100,000 km²; 4.8 million needed shelter |
| Effects | Landslides; rivers blocked by sand/mud → lakes formed |

## 8. Case Study: Japan Tsunami 2011

- Magnitude **9.0** earthquake below Pacific Ocean off east coast of Japan
- Triggered **tsunami** with waves up to **40 m** high
- > 15,500 people died
- Caused damage to **Fukushima nuclear power plant** → nuclear leakage

---

## 9. Preventive & Remedial Measures 預防及補救措施

### Preventive Measures (before):
| Measure | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Monitoring & early warning systems** | Track seismic waves; send phone alerts |
| **Land use planning** | Avoid building dangerous facilities in earthquake-prone areas; set up evacuation routes |
| **Earthquake-proof buildings** | Prevent collapse (e.g. Transamerica Pyramid) |
| **Education & earthquake drills** | Increase awareness; reduce losses and injuries |

### Remedial Measures (after):
| Measure | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Well-trained rescue teams** | Increase number of survivors |
| **Temporary shelters & aid** | Provide food, shelter, financial aid to rebuild |
