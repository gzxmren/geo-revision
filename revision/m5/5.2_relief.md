# Unit 5.2 — How Can We Describe the Relief of a Place?
## 如何描述一個地方的地勢？

---

## 1. Relief 地勢

**Relief** = the shape of the land, including **height** and **steepness** (地勢 = 土地的形狀，包括高度和陡峭程度)

---

## 2. Showing Relief on a Map 在地圖上表示地勢

Three methods:

### A. Colouring 着色法
- **Brown** = highland areas
- **Green** = lowland areas

### B. Contouring 等高線法
- **Contour line 等高線**: A line joining places of the **same height**
- **Vertical Interval (V.I.) 垂直間距**: The difference in height between **two consecutive** contour lines
  - e.g. V.I. = 20 m means each contour line differs by 20 m from the next

### C. Conventional Signs 慣用符號
- **Trigonometric station 三角網測站** (symbol: triangle with dot): Shows exact height on hilltops
- **Spot height 高程點** (symbol: • or +): Shows exact height at a specific point

---

## 3. Drawing a Cross Section 繪製剖面圖

A cross section shows the relief along a line on a contour map.

### Steps:
1. Place a piece of paper along the line P–Q on the map
2. Mark where contour lines cross the edge of the paper
3. Transfer marks to the bottom of graph paper
4. Plot heights according to the **vertical scale**
5. Join the points with a smooth line
6. Add a title

### Key Calculation Formulas

#### Vertical Exaggeration (V.E.) 垂直放大率
```
V.E. = Vertical Scale (VS) ÷ Horizontal Scale (HS)
```
- VS = 1 cm : X m (the scale you choose for height)
- HS = map scale (e.g. 1 cm : 50 m on the map)
- V.E. > 1 means the height is exaggerated to make it visible

#### Slope Gradient 坡度
```
Gradient = Vertical Rise ÷ Horizontal Distance
```
Usually expressed as "1 in X" — e.g. 1 in 5 means for every 5 m horizontally, the height rises by 1 m.

---

## 4. Slope Steepness from Contour Lines

| Contour Line Spacing | Slope Characteristic |
|---------------------|---------------------|
| **Closely packed** (close together) | **Steep slope** |
| **Widely spaced** (far apart) | **Gentle slope** |

---

## 5. Common Relief Features on Contour Maps

| Feature | Description | Contour Pattern |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Conical hill** 圓錐丘 | Rounded hill; height increases toward centre | Concentric circles, innermost highest |
| **Flat-topped hill** 平頂山 | Flat summit, steep sides | Innermost contour wide/flat |
| **Ridge** 山脊 | Elongated highland | Contours elongated, enclosing highest area |
| **Spur** 山咀 | Ridge-like projection descending from highland | Contours pointing downhill | 
| **Valley** 山谷 | Low-lying area between hills | Contours pointing uphill; river often in middle |
| **Saddle** 山口 / 鞍部 | Low point between two higher areas | Two high areas with low ground between |
| **Cliff** 懸崖 | Very steep/vertical slope | Contour lines merging or crossing |
| **Convex slope** 凸坡 | Slope gradient **increases** downhill | Contours closely packed in upper part, wider in lower part |
| **Concave slope** 凹坡 | Slope gradient **decreases** downhill | Contours widely spaced in upper part, closer in lower part |

---

## 6. Relief of Hong Kong 香港的地勢

| Type | Characteristics | Examples |
|------|----------------|----------|
| **Upland** 高地 | **Hilly** relief; much land > 200 m | Tai Mo Shan, Lantau Peak |
| **Lowland** 低地 | **Few** natural lowlands (river valleys & coastal plains) | Yuen Long Plain (largest in NW New Territories) |
| **Reclaimed land** 填海地 | Artificial lowlands created by **reclamation** | Tsim Sha Tsui, Sha Tin, Cheung Sha Wan |
| **Coastline** 海岸線 | **Long** and **indented**; many **bays** and **offshore islands** | Cheung Chau, Lamma Island |

## 📝 Exam Tips 考试提示

1. **Cross section drawing**: Likely in exam — practice drawing, labeling horizontal/vertical scales
2. **V.E. calculation**: Know the formula VS ÷ HS — convert units carefully (m to cm or vice versa)!
3. **Gradient**: Calculate as **Vertical Rise ÷ Horizontal Distance**; express as 1:X
4. **Relief features**: Be able to identify from contour patterns — ridges, spurs, valleys, saddles are common exam questions
5. **HK relief**: Know at least one example for upland, lowland, and reclaimed land
