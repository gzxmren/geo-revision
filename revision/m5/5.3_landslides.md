# Unit 5.3 — What Are the Causes and Effects of Landslides?
## 山泥傾瀉的原因和影響

---

## 1. What is a Landslide? 什麼是山泥傾瀉？

A landslide occurs when a **mass of soil and rocks** of all sizes moves down a slope **rapidly** due to **gravity**.

- Occurs on **steep slopes**
- Driven primarily by **gravity**

---

## 2. Slope Stability 斜坡穩定性

### Two Key Forces:

| Force | Description | Effect |
|-------|-------------|--------|
| **Driving force** 推動力 | Pulls slope materials **downhill** under gravity | Increases = LESS stable |
| **Resisting force** 阻抗力 | Prevents slope materials from moving downhill | Increases = MORE stable |

**When driving force > resisting force → LANDSLIDE occurs**

### Factors Affecting the Forces:

| Force | Factor | Explanation |
|-------|--------|-------------|
| Driving force | **Slope gradient** (坡度) | Steeper slope = greater driving force |
| Driving force | **Weight of slope materials** | Heavier materials = greater tendency to fall |
| Resisting force | **Cohesion** of slope materials (黏聚力) | Higher cohesion = more resistant |
| Resisting force | **Force of friction** (摩擦力) | Higher friction = more resistant |

---

## 3. Causes of Landslides in Hong Kong

### Natural Causes:

| Factor | How It Affects Stability |
|--------|-------------------------|
| **Heavy rainfall** 暴雨 | ⬆️ Water adds weight → ⬆️ Driving force; Water seeps in → ⬇️ Cohesion → ⬇️ Resisting force |
| **Large slope gradient** 大坡度 | ⬆️ Driving force |
| **Weathering** 風化 | Weakens rocks, reduces cohesion and friction → ⬇️ Resisting force |
| **Strong weathering** in HK's climate | HK's **hot and humid** climate accelerates chemical weathering of **granite** |

### Human Causes:

| Factor | How It Affects Stability |
|--------|-------------------------|
| **Cutting slopes** to build roads / buildings | ⬆️ Slope gradient → ⬆️ Driving force; removes vegetation |
| **Poor maintenance** of man-made slopes | ⬇️ Resisting force (cracks let water seep in, drainage blocked) |
| **Removing vegetation** | Roots no longer bind soil → ⬇️ Resisting force |

---

## 4. Hong Kong Context

- Many areas in HK are made of **granite** — easily **weathered** in HK's hot, humid climate
- Landslides are **frequent in summer** (rainy season with typhoons and rainstorms)
- Landslides can occur after **prolonged rainfall** or **days of heavy rain**

---

## 5. Preventive Measures 預防措施

### Reduce Driving Force:
| Measure | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| Building a **drainage channel** 排水渠 | Divert water away from slope to reduce weight |
| Installing a **rain gauge** 雨量計 | Monitor rainfall; issue warnings when threshold is reached |

### Increase Resisting Force:
| Measure | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Soil nailing** 泥釘 | Insert steel bars into slope → stabilize soil |
| Building a **retaining wall** 護土牆 | Provide support to the slope |
| **Shotcreting** (covering slope with gunite/concrete) 噴漿 | Seal slope surface → prevent water seepage |

### Prevent Materials from Rushing Down:
| Measure | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| Building a **debris / catch fence** 攔泥壩 | Catch falling materials before they reach roads/buildings |

### Other Measures:
- **Landslip warning system** issued by **Geotechnical Engineering Office ( GEO )** 
- Regular slope inspection
- Upgrading substandard man-made slopes
- Restricting development on steep slopes

---

## 6. Effects of Landslides 影響

- Block roads and highways → disrupt transport
- Damage or destroy buildings
- Injuries and loss of life
- Economic losses
- Disruption to daily life

---

## 📝 Exam Tips 考试提示

1. **Driving force vs Resisting force**: Be able to explain both, and what happens when one exceeds the other
2. **Natural vs Human causes**: Know which causes belong to which category
3. **Heavy rainfall = double effect**: Increases driving force AND decreases resisting force
4. **Preventive measures**: Know which measures reduce driving force vs which increase resisting force
5. **Case examples**: Remember HK examples — e.g., Tuen Mun landslide (7 June 2008), Tung Chung landslides
