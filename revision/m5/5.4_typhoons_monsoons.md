# Unit 5.4 — Why Does Most of Asia Suffer from Strong Winds and Heavy Rain in Summer?
## 為什麼亞洲大部分地區夏季會遭受強風和暴雨？

---

## 1. Weather vs Climate 天氣 vs 氣候

| | Weather 天氣 | Climate 氣候 |
|--|-------------|-------------|
| Definition | Condition of the air over a **short period** (hours to days) | Average weather conditions over **many years** (e.g. 30 years) |
| Example | "It is raining today" | "Hong Kong has hot, wet summers" |

### Elements of Weather / Climate:
| Element | What It Measures | Unit |
|---------|-----------------|------|
| **Air temperature** 氣溫 | Amount of heat in the air | °C or °F |
| **Wind direction & speed** 風向及風速 | Movement of air | m/s (speed); N/E/S/W (direction) |
| **Air pressure** 氣壓 | Weight of air | hPa (hectopascal) |
| **Precipitation** 降水 | Amount of water falling (rain, snow, etc.) | mm |
| **Relative humidity** 相對濕度 | Amount of moisture in the air | % |
| **Sunshine duration** 日照時間 | Total time of sunshine | hours |

## 2. Climatic Graph 氣候圖

- **Line graph** = monthly mean temperature
- **Bar graph** = monthly rainfall
- Used to show the **climate characteristics** of a place

## 3. Climate of Hong Kong / South China

### Seasons:

| Season | Months | Characteristics |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| Spring | Mar–Apr | Mild and humid; foggy |
| Summer | May–Sep | **Hot and wet**; southerly winds; typhoons and rainstorms |
| Autumn | Oct–Nov | Warm and dry; fine and sunny |
| Winter | Dec–Feb | **Cool and dry**; strong northerly winds |

---

## 4. Monsoons 季風

**Monsoons** = winds that change direction **seasonally**, blowing from one direction in summer and the opposite in winter.

### Summer Monsoon (summer):
- Land heats up **quickly** → hot air **rises** → **low pressure** over land
- Sea heats up **slowly** → cooler air **sinks** → **high pressure** over sea
- Wind blows from **sea → land** (onshore winds 向岸風)
- South-west / south-east summer monsoons → carry **moisture** → **wet summers**

### Winter Monsoon (winter):
- Land loses heat **quickly** → cold air **sinks** → **high pressure** over land
- Sea loses heat **slowly** → warm air **rises** → **low pressure** over sea
- Wind blows from **land → sea** (offshore winds 離岸風)
- North / north-west / north-east winter monsoons → **dry** → **cool, dry winters**

---

## 5. Extreme Summer Weather in South China

### A. Rainstorms 暴雨
- Caused by high temperatures + moist onshore monsoons
- Heavy rain → floods and landslides
- HK Observatory issues rainstorm warning signals based on rainfall rate:
  - Amber: > 30 mm/hr
  - Red: > 50 mm/hr
  - Black: > 70 mm/hr

### B. Typhoons 颱風
- A **tropical cyclone** (熱帶氣旋) = strong tropical storm
- A **low-pressure system** developing over **tropical waters** (warm oceans)

#### Conditions for Typhoon Formation:
1. Warm water with **surface temperatures > 26.5°C**
2. Abundant supply of **moist air**

#### Structure of a Typhoon:
| Part | Characteristics |
|------|----------------|
| **Eye** 風眼 | Centre — **calm**, no clouds |
| **Spiral rain bands** 螺旋雨帶 | Outside the eye — **strong winds**, heavy rain |
| **Low-pressure centre** 低壓中心 | At the centre of the typhoon |

#### Effects of Typhoons:
- Very strong winds
- Dense clouds, heavy rain
- **Storm surge** 風暴潮 (rise in sea level)
- Lightning

#### Origin and Movement:
- Form over Pacific Ocean near **Philippines**
- Move **westward** after formation
- Affect **eastern coast of Asia**

## 6. Reading Weather Charts 天氣圖

- **Isobars** 等壓線 = lines joining places with the **same air pressure**
- **Closely packed isobars** → **strong wind** (steep pressure gradient)
- **Widely spaced isobars** → **weak wind**

### Weather Chart Symbols:
- Air pressure: shown at stations (e.g. 1012 = 1012 hPa)
- Wind direction: arrow pointing **from** where wind blows
- Wind speed: feathers on arrow
- Precipitation: symbols for rain, showers, drizzle, snow, etc.

## 📝 Exam Tips 考试提示

1. **Weather vs Climate**: Be able to distinguish — short term vs long term
2. **Monsoon mechanism**: Understand WHY summer is wet and winter is dry (land vs sea, pressure differences, onshore vs offshore)
3. **Typhoon structure**: Know the parts (eye, spiral rain bands) and conditions needed for formation
4. **Weather chart reading**: Practice reading isobars, wind direction, temperature, precipitation
5. **Climatic graph drawing**: Be able to plot and describe temperature/rainfall patterns
