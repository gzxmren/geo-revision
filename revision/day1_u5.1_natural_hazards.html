1. 三个核心概念

这一节最核心的逻辑链条就一条：

🌋 Natural Event（自然事件）→ ⚠️ Natural Hazard（自然災害）→ 💥 Natural Disaster（自然災難）
  • Natural event = 由自然过程或力量引发的自然现象（如火山爆发） （不受人类控制的自然过程）
  • Natural hazard = 发生在人类居住区、威胁生命财产的自然事件 （害到人就是 hazard）
  • Natural disaster = 造成大量伤亡和严重财产损失的 hazard （损失巨大就是 disaster）
📌 考试常考：给你一个 scenario，问你它是 natural event / hazard / disaster？ 判断关键：是否影响人、损害程度多大。

🧠 记忆口诀
Geo = 地动山摇（地震、火山、山泥倾泻）
Meteo = 天上来的（台风、洪水、干旱）
Bio = 虫子闹的（蝗虫、虫害）
📌 考试常考：给你一个 hazard 名字，问属于哪一类。 或者反过来——"以下哪个是 meteorological hazard？"

3. 全球分布（Global Distribution）

📌 关键一句话：Distribution of natural hazards is UNEVEN（不均匀）。 — 这道题几乎必考，直接背这句。

具体分布规律

Hazard分布特点
Earthquake 地震沿 earthquake belts（地震带），如环太平洋
Volcanic eruption 火山爆发与地震带重叠；在太平洋呈 linear / clustered 分布
Typhoon 台风Tropical regions（23.5°N ~ 23.5°S）温暖海洋
Flood 洪水河边和沿海
Drought 干旱大陆内部、干燥区

Hong Kong 的情况

  • ✅ 有 typhoons 和 landslides
  • ❌ 没有 volcanic eruptions
  • ❌ 没有 major earthquakes（HK 不在板块交界处）

GIS 应用（了解即可）

  • GIS = Geographic Information System（地理信息系统）
  • 可以用 layers（图层） 在地图上叠加显示不同 hazards 的分布

MCQ
1. Which of the following is a geological hazard?
  • A. Typhoon
  • B. Flood
  • C. Landslide
  • D. Pest
✅ C. Landslide
Typhoon 和 Flood 是 meteorological hazard，Pest 是 biological hazard。Landslide 属于 geological hazard。
MCQ
2. Which of the following statements about natural hazards is/are correct?
1. They are naturally occurring phenomena.
2. Drought is an example of a meteorological hazard.
3. A very destructive typhoon can be called a natural disaster.
  • A. 1 and 2 only
  • B. 1 and 3 only
  • C. 2 and 3 only
  • D. 1, 2 and 3
✅ D. 1, 2 and 3
(1) 正确：natural hazards 是 naturally occurring
(2) 正确：drought 是 meteorological hazard
(3) 正确：造成巨大损失的 typhoon 可称为 natural disaster
MCQ
3. Which of the following is a biological hazard?
  • A. Volcanic eruption
  • B. Plague of locusts
  • C. Earthquake
  • D. Drought
✅ B. Plague of locusts（蝗祸）
Locusts 是 living organisms → 属于 biological hazard。
MCQ
4. Which of the following descriptions about the global distribution of natural hazards is correct?
  • A. Earthquakes are evenly distributed around the world
  • B. Typhoons mainly occur in polar regions
  • C. The distribution of natural hazards is uneven
  • D. Floods only occur in mountainous areas
✅ C. The distribution of natural hazards is uneven
这是本单元最常考的结论句。
MCQ
5. In which region do typhoons mainly form?
  • A. Polar regions
  • B. Temperate regions
  • C. Tropical regions (between 23.5°N and 23.5°S)
  • D. Along the equator only
✅ C. Tropical regions
台风需要温暖洋面（> 26.5°C），所以主要在热带形成。

Structured
8. State TWO differences between a natural hazard and a natural disaster. Give ONE example for each. （4分）
✅ Sample answer:
A natural hazard is a natural event that threatens human life and property, e.g. a typhoon approaching Hong Kong.

A natural disaster occurs when the hazard causes great loss of life and severe property damage, e.g. the 2008 Wenchuan earthquake (magnitude 8.0, >69,000 deaths).

评分要点：hazard = threaten（威胁）；disaster = great loss of life / severe damage（大量伤亡）；各给一个正确例子

Write
10. Describe the global distribution of typhoons. Use the words "uneven" and "tropical" in your answer. （4分）
✅ Sample answer:
The global distribution of typhoons is uneven. Typhoons are mainly found in tropical regions between 23.5°N and 23.5°S. They form over warm ocean waters and move westward. Countries in East and Southeast Asia, such as Japan, the Philippines, and Hong Kong, are frequently affected by typhoons.
