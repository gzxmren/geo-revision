Q4
______ prevents slope materials from sliding down. It is affected by the cohesion of slope materials and the force of friction.
  • A. Resisting force
  • B. Driving force
  • C. Rainwater
  • D. Gravity
✅ Answer: A. Resisting force（阻抗力）
📌 分析：
• 题目说"prevents slope materials from sliding down" → 阻止滑落的力
• 影响因素是 cohesion（黏聚力）和 friction（摩擦力）→ 这两者都影响 resisting force
• Resisting force ✅：阻止下滑，受 cohesion 和 friction 影响
• Driving force ❌：推动下滑，受坡度和重量影响
• Rainwater ❌：会加重 slope materials 并降低 cohesion
• Gravity ❌：产生 driving force 的主要来源

Q2(d)5 marks
Explain how summer monsoons are formed in South China.
✅ Model Answer:
  1. In summer, the land heats up quickly, causing the air above it to rise → low pressure forms over land.
  2. The sea heats up slowly, so the air above the sea stays cooler → high pressure forms over the ocean.
  3. Air flows from high pressure (sea) → low pressure (land), creating onshore winds.
  4. These onshore winds carry abundant moisture from the ocean.
  5. When the moist air rises over land and cools, it condenses → forms clouds → brings heavy rainfall.
📌 关键术语：land heats up quicker / low pressure / high pressure over sea / onshore wind / moisture / condensation
Reference: Unit 5.4 — Summer Monsoon Mechanism

Q4(c)(ii)2 marks
Work out the vertical exaggeration of the cross-section. Show your calculation.
V.E. Formula:
V.E. = Vertical Scale (VS) ÷ Horizontal Scale (HS)
Calculation:
• VS = 1 cm : 10 m = 1 cm : 1,000 cm (convert to same unit) → 1 : 1,000
• HS = Map scale = 1 : 5,000 (1 cm on map = 5,000 cm on ground)
• V.E. = 1,000 ÷ 5,000 = 0.2
📌 或写为 1 : 5（水平方向是垂直方向的 5 倍）
注意单位统一换算：所有长度用 cm 或 m。
Q4(d)(i)3 marks
Calculate the average gradient of the slope from A to the spot height 63.5.
Gradient Formula:
Gradient = Vertical Rise (VR) ÷ Horizontal Distance (HD)
• Vertical Rise = Height difference = 63.5 m − (height of A)
• Horizontal Distance = Map distance × Map scale
 = (measured cm on map) × 5,000 cm = result in cm → convert to m
• Gradient = VR (m) ÷ HD (m)
通常表达为 "1 in X"，如 "1 in 5" 表示水平每前进 5 m，高度上升 1 m。
Q4(d)(ii)3 marks
Calculate the average gradient of the slope from C to the spot height 63.5.
Same method as Q4(d)(i)：
Gradient = Vertical Rise ÷ Horizontal Distance
对比两条坡道的数值大小，值越小（如 1:5）表示越陡；值越大（如 1:20）表示越缓。
